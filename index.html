Hassen Family Assistant

A private, personal-use assistant for members of one household. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

With a family member's permission, it reads and, only when that person approves each action, writes to their own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, to help them manage email, schedules and documents.

Hassen Family Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
