Privacy Policy

Hassen Family Assistant · last updated 3 November 2026

Who uses it

Only members of one household, each connecting their own Google account. It is not available to the public.

Google data accessed

Gmail (read, send, labels), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Contacts (read-only), only for the account the user connects themselves.

How it is used

Only to carry out what the account holder asks: finding and summarising email, managing calendar events, and creating or editing documents. Sending email, changing or deleting events, and sharing or deleting files happen only after the account holder approves that specific action.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens and any working files are stored on the family's own computer and are not shared with other users.
  • To answer a request, relevant content is processed by the AI model provider the assistant runs on (Anthropic), solely to fulfil that request.
  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalised AI models.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, stored tokens and data are deleted.
